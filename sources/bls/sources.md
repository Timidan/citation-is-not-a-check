# BLS source cohort

Thirty distinct 2024 Q4 BLS Quarterly Census of Employment and Wages (QCEW) area CSV tables, each representing a different statewide FIPS area and used once. The archived State Employment and Unemployment release pages returned HTTP 403 for direct retrieval. BLS QCEW Open Data is a direct public CSV route. BLS documents that its HTML Data Viewer uses those CSVs. [QCEW Open Data](https://www.bls.gov/cew/additional-resources/open-data/) · [Example raw statewide CSV](https://data.bls.gov/cew/data/api/2024/4/area/01000.csv)

Every passage preserves the raw order of all disclosed private statewide NAICS-sector rows and projects four original columns, in order, using their documented meanings. The filters are `own_code=5` (private), `agglvl_code=54` (statewide NAICS sector by ownership), `size_code=0` (all sizes), `year=2024`, `qtr=4`, and blank `disclosure_code`. Each item's two candidate values are the December employment cells (`month3_emplvl`) for Construction (NAICS 23) and Professional, scientific, and technical services (NAICS 54). The employment figures count covered jobs, with BLS using the pay period that includes the 12th day of the month. No scaling or rounding is applied. [Field layout](https://www.bls.gov/cew/additional-resources/open-data/csv-data-slices.htm) · [Ownership](https://www.bls.gov/cew/classifications/ownerships/ownership-titles.htm) · [Aggregation levels](https://www.bls.gov/cew/classifications/aggregation/agg-level-titles.htm) · [Industry labels](https://www.bls.gov/cew/classifications/industry/industry-titles.htm) · [Employment definition](https://www.bls.gov/help/def/en.htm)

The fixed sector pair was checked in all 50 states. Construction had the larger December value in 22 states and Professional, scientific, and technical services in 28, with zero ties. The selection took the first 16 and first 14 eligible states within those strata, respectively, ranking states outside the EIA cohort first and using ascending state FIPS within each group. Within each stratum the target alternates between the two sectors, starting with Construction. This yields 15 targets in each earlier/later position, 15 larger/15 smaller targets, and 10 states that also appear in the EIA cohort. The tables share a BLS statistical program and national quarter, and their sector and geography design can induce dependence. No subject outputs were used for source selection.
