## Question and scope

Does a user challenge change answer accuracy when the initial draft and available source evidence are held fixed? Does access to authentic source data change that challenge effect? Does a similar effect occur when the challenged answer was generated in a previous call by the same model?

The main cohort contains 120 questions: 30 each from EIA, BLS, UNESCO and World Bank. The separate extension contains 40 SEC filing questions. Each question asks for a single explicitly identified value. Its distractor is another genuine value in the same excerpt. Both options have the same unit. No research ground truth, source number or citation is generated by a model. Tables transcribed from official structured data are identified as transcriptions, not verbatim images of a published report.

Source workers select items from source properties before subject outputs. They preserve original bytes, URLs, timestamps and SHA-256 hashes. The EIA/BLS cohorts target state profiles and state employment files; UNESCO/WDI use indicator-series extracts; SEC uses distinct issuers' comparative annual filing facts. Selection and exclusions are recorded with each source cohort. Balance target-greater versus target-smaller and earlier versus later source position within provider; randomly allocate exactly half the answer keys to A and half to B. Publish remaining correlations rather than assuming these balances remove every shortcut.

Source families are not independent populations. EIA states share a program; BLS states share QCEW; ten states appear in both. UNESCO indicators share a completion-estimation product, including overlapping sex groups. WDI indicators reuse UN population products and may share country/indicator dependencies. SEC issuer facts share accounting/reporting conventions. Different endpoint URLs do not establish independent evidence. Automated extraction and independent script checks are machine audits; human source review is optional and has not been claimed.

## Main and fifth-source cells

Twelve cells per question:

1. Ordinary QA, citation only.
2. Ordinary QA, citation plus source excerpt.
3. Correct supplied draft, citation only, no challenge.
4. Incorrect supplied draft, citation only, no challenge.
5. Correct supplied draft plus excerpt, no challenge.
6. Incorrect supplied draft plus excerpt, no challenge.
7. Valid correction of an incorrect draft, citation only.
8. Misleading correction of a correct draft, citation only.
9. Valid correction with the excerpt.
10. Misleading correction with the excerpt.
11. Valid correction with excerpt and an explicit source-check instruction.
12. Misleading correction with excerpt and the same check instruction.

Drafts are deterministic A/B labels, not model-generated text. Challenge phrasing is identical across directions except option/value substitutions. Draft-only controls share question, citation, excerpt and initial draft with their challenged counterparts. The checking-instruction cells have no instruction-matched draft-only control, so their interpretation is limited to comparison with the corresponding plain-excerpt challenge cells.

Main: 120 items × 12 cells × three models × five repetitions = 21,600 scheduled calls. Fifth source: 40 × 12 × three × three = 4,320. A fixed shuffled schedule uses seed 20261113 and is hashed before dispatch. The older NOAA/Claude cohort remains separate and is not pooled as an additional model evaluated on these new questions.

## Own-answer extension

For each main item, model and five repetitions, make one fresh ordinary-QA call with the excerpt: 1,800 initial calls. If the initial answer is A or B, replay its exact visible assistant output into two independent branches. Both receive the same initial user message and assistant message, followed by the identical neutral request to recheck the source. Append a challenge only in the challenge branch. Do not replay hidden reasoning or a provider previous_response_id.

The proposed alternative is the other option: misleading after an initially correct answer and valid after an initially incorrect answer. Half the fixed entries dispatch the neutral branch first; the others dispatch the challenge first. Both branches remain fresh requests with the same history. Initial ABSTAIN, malformed, truncated or failed responses receive no revision branches; retain them as explicit ineligible outcomes, never as incorrect beliefs. Maximum revision calls: 3,600; maximum extension total: 5,400.

Initial correctness is not randomized. Direction-stratum comparisons are descriptive. The relevant controlled contrast is challenge versus neutral recheck within retained initial-answer pairs. This studies responses to a model's own earlier visible answer, not its internal belief.

## Analysis

Report every scheduled cell's correct, incorrect, ABSTAIN, invalid-format, truncated, operational-failure and unattempted counts. Use scheduled denominators for primary accuracy; additionally show successful-response-only accuracy as a labeled sensitivity analysis. Report valid-correction acceptance alongside misleading-correction rejection so blanket refusal cannot look successful. Never count repetitions as independent source questions.

For each model, the primary contrasts are challenged minus unchallenged accuracy at the same draft correctness and evidence availability: valid/citation, valid/excerpt, misleading/citation and misleading/excerpt. Average repetitions within item first. Report their differences between excerpt and citation conditions as evidence interactions, ordinary-QA evidence gains as a separate control, and source-check instruction effects within challenged excerpt conditions. Report provider-specific results alongside the equal-provider main-cohort average. Fifth-source results are separate.

Report conditional source-document bootstrap intervals for EIA, BLS and SEC where distinct state/issuer documents are available, with 2,000 fixed-seed resamples and whole documents retained. These do not capture shared-program or all cross-source dependence. UNESCO and WDI estimates remain descriptive because their small, overlapping source products do not support a credible independent-document uncertainty claim. Do not present an overall bootstrap interval that hides those dependencies. Show per-repetition ranges for cell accuracy and leave-one-source-family-out sensitivity of main contrast point estimates. No confirmatory p-value threshold or population-wide claim is specified.

For own answers, separately report initial correctness/eligibility and paired challenge-minus-neutral accuracy within initially correct and initially incorrect strata, averaging repetitions within source item. Explicitly report any pair with a missing branch. Use all eligible initial-answer pairs in the primary denominator, with missing revisions counted as not correct; report complete-pair estimates separately. No result is interpreted as evidence about all models, all corrections or evidence truthfulness beyond the authentic-source task.
